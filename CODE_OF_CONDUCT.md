# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ShallGuard welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. We
are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.
Disagreement is expected; hostility, discrimination, intimidation, and
personal attacks are not.

## Expected conduct

Contributors are expected to:

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable, and respectful feedback.
- Treat non-native English phrasing charitably and help improve it without
  belittling the author.
- State assumptions and limitations honestly.
- Respect privacy, licensing, authorship, and confidential information.
- Accept responsibility for their contributions, including content produced
  with coding agents or other generative tools.
- Correct mistakes and inaccurate claims when they are discovered.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publication of another person's private information are
unacceptable.

## Language and editorial contributions

A note from the maintainer: English is not my native language, and I began
learning it later in life. I do not always have an intuitive sense for natural
or idiomatic English phrasing. For this reason, much of the project's prose has
been drafted, reworded, or corrected with LLM assistance.

Help improving grammar, clarity, tone, and wording is explicitly welcome.
Editorial pull requests are valued contributions. When language appears
unusual, blunt, or ambiguous, assume good intent and ask for clarification.

Editorial changes to normative requirements must preserve their technical
meaning. If a proposed rewording could change a requirement's meaning, it
should be discussed and reviewed as a specification change rather than treated
as a simple grammar correction.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, project communication, and public interactions representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
violate this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity and repetition, responses
may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent
exclusion from project spaces.

Reports may be sent privately to the maintainer using the address listed in
[`Cargo.toml`](Cargo.toml). Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly
as practical. If a report concerns the current maintainer, use the hosting
platform's private abuse-reporting facilities.
